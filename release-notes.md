Cursora 0.4.0 fills any kind of form, with any model, and Cursora is now open source.

New
- Fill this form now handles drop-down lists, radio buttons, check boxes and date boxes, not only text boxes. A check box on its own, like "I agree to the terms", is never ticked for you.
- Forms are understood however they ask: "Name", "Contact No.", "DOB", "Pincode", "Please enter your email" and nearly 200 other common wordings, Hindi ones too, match your details right away, without asking the AI model.
- Your details are fitted to each box: your first name from your full name, a date in the box's own format (DD/MM/YYYY and others), and the option that matches your answer ("F" picks Female). Your details still never go to a model.
- Works much better with small models on your own computer: with the built-in 1.5B model, wrong matches on real form labels went from 7 in 48 to none.
- The plan shows exactly what will be typed in each box before you press Fill.
- Cursora is now free and open source under the GPL-3.0 license: github.com/priyansh1210/cursora

Fixed
- Google Forms' "Required question" mark no longer stops a box from matching its detail.
