# Code of conduct

Cursora follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, welcome newcomers, assume good intent, and keep feedback about the work, not the person. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project meets.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, [@priyansh1210](https://github.com/priyansh1210), privately through GitHub. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, or block people who break these rules.
