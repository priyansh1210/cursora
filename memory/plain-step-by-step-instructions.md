When the user must do something at the PC (UAC prompts, Settings checkboxes, tray menus), give one short numbered step at a time, in plain words, saying where to click and what they should see. Avoid jargon such as "elevated", "listbox" or "autocomplete".

**Why:** on 2026-10-14/15 the user asked "explain me in easy way what to do" and "i am confused which list" when the steps were dense, and declined the Narrator check.

**How to apply:** split a check into its smallest parts, and ask about one part per question. Related: [[after-restart-human-checks]].
